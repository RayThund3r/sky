🎤 About Snowy

Snowy Kyt is a Ugandan solo musician who passionately blends Afrobeats and world music into rhythms that move both soul and body. As a cultural ambassador, he’s journeyed across countries sharing African dances, folk songs, and instruments—uniting people through the universal language of music and movement. His tracks often spark viral trends and reflect the vibrant pulse of East Africa’s music scene.
